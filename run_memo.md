# 🗡️ Slay the Spire Agent 战略黑板 (Run Strategic Blackboard)

> 此文档由 MacroSession (宏观战略) 与 CombatSession (战术实战) 共同维护，实时同步全局流派规划与战况反思。

## 🎯 宏观流派与作战锦囊 (Macro Strategic Briefing)
- **流派定位**: Act 1 Burst / Transition
- **致胜终端**: Front-load enough single-target damage to kill Act 1 elites and hallway fights before they can leverage their scaling, then pivot into a defined scaling engine in Act 2.
- **实战打牌偏好**: Be aggressive early—play Bash on the biggest threat, then dump damage (Carnage, Strikes) to end fights fast; only block when facing lethal or unavoidable big hits, and save defends for weak/multi-hit turns. Aim to spend energy on damage first.
- **下步抓牌/删牌重点**: Prioritize premium single-target damage (Carnage, Heavy Blade, Uppercut), early AoE clear (Cleave, Whirlwind), and efficient block/draw; avoid skill-heavy or slow powers without support, and plan to remove Strikes at campfires/shop once the core engine is found.
