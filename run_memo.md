## 🎯 宏观流派与作战锦囊 (Macro Strategic Briefing)
- **流派定位**: Early Transition / High Strength Lean
- **致胜终端**: Overwhelm Act 1 elites with high frontloaded single-target damage and limited AoE before they can execute their scaling patterns.
- **实战打牌偏好**: Play aggressively: use Bash on the priority target, then spend remaining energy on damage; block only when lethal incoming damage is unavoidable.
- **下步抓牌/删牌重点**: Prioritize premium frontloaded attacks (Carnage, Iron Wave, Heavy Blade, Wild Strike) and at least one AoE (Cleave, Immolate); skip mediocre filler and avoid over-diluting the deck.
